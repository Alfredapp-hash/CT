Finding Your Self-Worth

Paperback · 2024 · ISBN 9798330546138

The modern woman is asked to be the mother, the partner, the employee, the daughter, and the friend — perfectly, and all at once. Finding Your Self-Worth, published as Finding Yourself, names that overwhelm without calling it weakness, and walks toward self-compassion, boundaries, and the shift from “I should” to “I want.”

From the pages

It’s okay to admit it. We’re overwhelmed. And it’s not a sign of weakness.
