A story for children

Finding Drake’s Feather

Paperback · 2024 · ISBN 9798330529551

Saying goodbye is never easy, especially for young hearts. Finding Drake’s Feather follows Mama Hen and her ducklings after Papa Drake is gone. Together they talk about sadness, play his games, and keep a feather so his love has a place to live. Written for children who have experienced loss, and for the families who support them.

From the pages

“Papa Drake’s love will always be with us. He will always be in our hearts. And we will always remember him.”
