Faith & purpose

Rooted in Purpose

Hardcover · 2025 · ISBN 9798348592240

Purpose, Courtney writes, begins quietly — long before we believe we are ready. Rooted in Purpose walks through burial and growth: storms that strengthen roots, seasons of waiting, the courage to prune, and the faith that a life is a garden already being tended. Each chapter closes with a lesson and an affirmation.

From the pages

“Your purpose is not lost. It’s growing quietly, even when you cannot see it.”
